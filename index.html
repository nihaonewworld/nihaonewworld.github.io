01 / About

About Me

Bioinformatics engineer focused on NGS data analysis and interpretation.

My work covers multi-omics — epigenomics, transcriptomics, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomics. I am involved throughout the data lifecycle: experimental design, sequencing data processing, statistical analysis, and biological interpretation.

A core part of my work is multi-layer data integration — combining epigenomic, transcriptomic, and single-cell evidence into coherent biological conclusions. My analytical frameworks are designed to be statistically rigorous and reproducible, so that findings can be revisited, extended, and reused across projects.

I maintain bio_know_base, a GitHub repository that consolidates my bioinformatics knowledge — methodological summaries, analysis scripts, and reusable templates accumulated through hands-on NGS projects.

Currently exploring AI-driven drug discovery (AIDD) and virtual cell technologies.
