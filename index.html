03 / APPROACH

A clear path through the data.

I keep the biological question and the analysis decisions connected.

  1. 01

    Define

    Start with the biological question, sample groups and replicates. Define the comparison and the outputs that would make the result useful.

  2. 02

    Check

    Review sample metadata, quality summaries and reference compatibility. Make batch structure and potential confounders visible before interpreting differences.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    Choose methods that fit the question and data. Keep parameters and analysis decisions traceable, then connect statistical results with annotations and evidence across assays.

  4. 04

    Interpret

    Bring figures and biological context together. Distinguish associations from mechanisms, make limitations explicit and identify questions for follow-up.

MAKING RESULTS READABLE

Keep the reasoning with the result.

Data context
Sample definitions, quality observations and the comparison being made.
Analysis decisions
Methods, parameters and assumptions needed to understand the analysis.
